How I Can Help

Want to use AI practically and safely? At GitHub during the Copilot era, I saw how AI can improve developer productivity at scale. I can help identify high-value use cases, implement responsibly, and ship AI-powered features customers use.

Need to scale reliably? I've worked on high-traffic systems including bookdepository.com, Amazon.co.uk, iCloud, and GitHub's Container Registry (ghcr.io). I can help remove bottlenecks and scale without sacrificing reliability.

Across Oracle, Amazon, Apple, and GitHub, I've worked in different engineering cultures and delivery models. I can improve CI/CD, testing, and developer workflows so your team ships faster with fewer regressions.

I help teams design for 99.9%+ uptime. At GitHub, I served as Availability Champion for Actions, Packages, and Pages. I can help with resilience design, recovery readiness, and post-incident improvement.

Modernizing a legacy platform? I can help plan and deliver safe, gradual migrations. Through work at Oracle and Amazon, I've led replatforming efforts without disrupting core business operations.

I can advise, but I'm still an IC at heart. If you need support shipping a complex feature, I can embed with your team and help deliver it.
